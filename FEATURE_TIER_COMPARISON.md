## 🎯 Recommended Tier by Use Case

### Individual Users (Solo)
- **Casual User** (1-2x/week) → **Free** (No need to pay)
- **Regular User** (daily) → **Premium** ($19/mo) — Best value
- **Power User** (multiple agents, heavy API) → **Premium** ($19/mo) + Add-ons

### Teams (2+ people)
- **Small Team** (2-5 people) → **Premium** ($19/mo each) — Flexible
- **Growing Team** (6-20 people) → **Enterprise** ($99/mo per seat) — Team features
- **Large Org** (21+ people) → **Enterprise** (Volume discount available)

### Developers (API-focused)
- **Hobbyist** (side projects) → **Free** (100 req/day)
- **Indie Dev** (small app) → **Starter** ($29/mo, 10K req/month)
- **Startup** (growing app) → **Pro** ($99/mo, 100K req/month)
- **Enterprise** (production app) → **Custom** (Volume pricing)

## 💡 Feature Gating Philosophy

### What to Gate (Premium Features)
✅ **Unlimited usage** (remove daily/monthly limits)  
✅ **Advanced capabilities** (premium voices, custom agents)  
✅ **Analytics & insights** (trends, reports, ROI)  
✅ **Collaboration** (team features, shared workspaces)  
✅ **Integrations** (more connections, more actions)  
✅ **Priority support** (faster response, dedicated help)  

### What to Keep Free (Core Experience)
✅ **Core functionality** (voice, journal, 1 agent)  
✅ **Basic features** (enough to prove value)  
✅ **No hidden costs** (transparent limits)  
✅ **No feature surprises** (don't take away what users have)  

**Golden Rule**: Free tier should be genuinely useful, not a trial disguised as free

## 🚨 Pricing Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Too many tiers** (4+ tiers confuse users)  
❌ **Unclear differences** (free vs paid looks the same)  
❌ **Hidden fees** (surprise charges lose trust)  
❌ **No free trial** (removes risk, increases conversions)  
❌ **Complex pricing** (per-seat + per-feature = confusion)  
❌ **No annual option** (miss out on committed customers)  
❌ **Overpricing** (compare to competitors, be reasonable)  
❌ **Underpricing** (leaves money on the table, devalues product)  

✅ **Do**: Simple, transparent, fair. Optimize based on data.
