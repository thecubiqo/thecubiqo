=================================================================
HOW IT WORKS
=================================================================

CAPSULE FORMAT:
  green:trade:tutor,science
  yellow:movies
  red:company:date

STAGED MATCHING:
  Stage 1: Color match (40 points) - REQUIRED
  Stage 2: Intent match (30 points)
  Stage 3: Keyword match (30 points)
  Total >= 70 = Proactive suggestion

GEOFENCING:
  Dating/hookup = Geofenced
  IT/science = Not geofenced
  Default radius: 25km
