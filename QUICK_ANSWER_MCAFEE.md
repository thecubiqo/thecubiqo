# Quick Answer: Is CubiQo Comparable to McAfee?

## TL;DR

**YES and NO** - They're comparable in security quality but serve **different layers**:

- **McAfee**: Protects devices (antivirus, endpoint security)
- **CubiQo**: Protects web apps (API security, fraud detection, privacy)

**They're COMPLEMENTARY, not competitive** - use both together.
